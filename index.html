About Me

I'm a cloud engineer and AWS re/Start graduate based in Johannesburg, driven by a deep fascination with scalable systems, serverless architecture, and the power of automation to transform how businesses operate.

Cloud computing captivates me because of its ability to scale up or down within minutes, while remaining cost-efficient through pay-as-you-go models. What excites me most is how AWS brings together compute, storage, AI, and machine learning into one unified ecosystem.

I also operate at the command line — navigating systems, managing services, editing configurations, and deploying applications with precision. I sometimes call myself an "Operating System Wizard" because the CLI gives me a deeper sense of control over every layer of infrastructure.

I currently serve as a Cloud Developer Intern at the Special Investigating Unit (SIU), where I am building toward a career as a Solutions Architect with a mission to engineer cloud systems with performance, security and scalability at their core.

// ARCHITECTURE PHILOSOPHY

Design Principles

01 ——
Scalability First
Systems must be designed to scale horizontally without architectural changes. Build for tomorrow's load, not today's.
02 ——
Security by Design
Least privilege, encryption at rest and in transit, and IAM precision — security is not an afterthought, it is the foundation.
03 ——
Cost-Conscious Infrastructure
Every architecture decision has a financial dimension. Serverless, pay-as-you-go, and free-tier optimization are engineering disciplines.
04 ——
Automation Everywhere
If a human is doing it repeatedly, it should be automated. CI/CD, infrastructure as code, and event-driven systems replace manual operations.
05 ——
High Availability Thinking
Design for failure. Multi-region redundancy, graceful degradation, and chaos-resilient architectures keep systems alive when things go wrong.
